Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version

Your logs stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps, and nothing is sold to anyone. To improve the app we collect anonymous usage stats, and you can switch them off.

What you log

Everything you log in Lifestyle Island (habits, amounts, times) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Lifestyle Island reads steps, walking/running distance, workouts, sleep, weight and mindful minutes, and uses them only to shape your island. It never writes to Health, never uploads Health data, never stores it in iCloud, and never uses it for advertising or marketing.

Anonymous usage stats

To see which features help and where people get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. They're tied to a random ID created on your phone, never to your name, email or Apple ID.

They include which screens and features you use (for example "finished the goals step", "opened Craving SOS"), which habits you chose or logged (for example "water", "vaping"), app and iOS version, device model, language, time zone, and crash reports.

They never include amounts (how many cigarettes, drinks or hours), anything you type (your island or mayor name, your "why", notes), or any Apple Health data. We don't sell this data or use it for advertising. The events are processed by PostHog in the United States. Because they aren't tied to you, we can't look them up by name; turning them off in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats stops collection immediately. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland they're off unless you turn them on.

Widgets and Siri

The home-screen widget and Siri shortcuts exchange data with the app through a private App Group container on your device.

Sharing

Clips, share cards and island links are created only when you tap Share, and go only where you send them. An island link contains your island's name, mayor name, streak, theme, decorations and current look (for example how smoggy it is), which reflects your habits. It contains no logs and no Health data.

Purchases

Island Plus purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The anonymous usage stats record which plan was chosen (for example "yearly, free trial") so we know which offers work.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Children

Lifestyle Island is not directed at children and includes references to alcohol and nicotine.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or a request about your data? Email lifestyleislandsupport@gmail.com.
