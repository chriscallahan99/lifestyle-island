Streaks

How do I keep my streak going?

Log anything on a day, or tap Check in on a quiet day. That's it: one tap counts.

I slipped and logged a cigarette, vape or drink. Did I lose my streak?

No. Logging a slip honestly always keeps your streak. It just doesn't earn the day's rewards (the overnight delivery, streak parties or a storm shield); logging something good or a clean day does.

What are storm shields?

If you miss a day, a storm shield covers it automatically so your streak survives. You earn one for every 7 days in a row. Everyone can hold 2; Island Plus holds 5 and adds a free one each week.

I'm traveling. Will a time zone change break my streak?

No. If flying east skips a date on the calendar, that day counts automatically and no shield is used.

How do I change my streak goal?

Tap the 🔥 streak chip at the top of the island, then Change next to your goal.

The island

How do overnight deliveries work?

Log a good habit (or a clean day) and your citizens build something overnight. Open the app the next morning to unwrap it. Once your collection is complete, deliveries give your decorations a golden makeover. Some decorations are seasonal and only arrive at certain times of year.

How do I add the widget?
  • Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  • Tap Edit (or +), then Add Widget.
  • Search for Lifestyle Island, pick a size and tap Add Widget.

Tapping a habit on the widget logs it without opening the app. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, and add your streak.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. The app follows your iPhone's text size: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size.

How do I change or turn off notifications?

In the app: Settings → Notifications. Each kind (daily reminder, streak about to end, deliveries, milestones, weekly recap) has its own switch.

Island Plus

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lifestyle Island → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. If you started a free trial, the app reminds you 2 days before it ends.

I bought Island Plus but it's not showing

In the app: Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have an offer code

In the app: Settings → Redeem a code.

Your data

Where are my logs stored?

Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts. Your logs are included in your regular iPhone backup, and deleting the app deletes them.

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health?

Connect in the app: Settings → Connect Apple Health. It only reads (steps, workouts, sleep, weight, mindful minutes) and never uploads Health data. To disconnect: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Lifestyle Island.

How do I turn off usage stats?

In the app: Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. Nothing is sent after that. See the privacy policy for exactly what's collected.

Need more than an app?

Lifestyle Island is a motivation tool, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about quitting. If you drink heavily, don't stop suddenly without medical advice: alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous. Free, confidential support in the US:

  • Quit smoking or vaping: 1-800-QUIT-NOW, or text DITCHVAPE to 88709, or smokefree.gov
  • Drinking: SAMHSA Helpline 1-800-662-4357, or Rethinking Drinking
  • Struggling or in crisis: call or text 988

These are also in the app under Settings → Get help. Outside the US, your doctor or local health service can point you to support near you.
